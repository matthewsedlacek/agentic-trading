## Why Escalated

1. **OCO protection unavailable for fractional positions.** All screen-passing candidates (market cap ≥ $2B, avg volume ≥ 500K) are priced well above $25. Buying $25 notional of CB (~$368/share) yields ~0.068 fractional shares. The Robinhood connector's OCO advanced-order tool requires **whole-share quantity** — fractional positions cannot be paired with broker-side OCO protective orders. POLICY §3 requires broker-side exits on every entry. Auto-executing a buy without that protection would violate the policy.

2. **Per-trade cap applied to whole-share alternative.** Buying 1 whole share of CB at ~$368 would cost ~$368 — well above the $25 per-trade cap.

**Operator action required:** Decide whether to (a) accept fractional entry with separate (non-OCO) stop-market + limit-sell orders placed manually, (b) await a price pullback to a level where the whole-share thesis can be executed with OCO protection, or (c) approve a one-time above-cap entry.
