# Proposal: JNJ — Johnson & Johnson (2026-07-30 run)

## Why Escalated

Buying power insufficient for a whole-share position ($0.29 available; 1 share of JNJ = $255.71).
Robinhood supports fractional shares only for market orders — stop-loss and limit-sell (take-profit)
orders require whole-share quantities. Policy requires broker-side protective orders at entry.
A buy without a defined broker-side exit is prohibited. Both spend caps ($25/trade, $50/day) are
technically satisfied for a fractional buy, but broker-side stop placement is not feasible on a
fractional lot. Escalating for manual execution when buying power is replenished.
