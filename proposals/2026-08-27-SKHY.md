## Thesis
I own SKHY because SK Hynix holds an estimated ~70% market share in HBM3E (High Bandwidth Memory), the highest-margin chip category, supplied exclusively or preferentially to Nvidia for AI accelerators (H100/H200/B100/B200). HBM capacity cannot be ramped quickly — clean room buildout takes 18–24 months — so HBM pricing remains supply-constrained through at least 2027. The stock trades at ~10x earnings despite being supply-side gated in the fastest-growing semiconductor sub-segment.

## Falsifier
I am wrong if Samsung closes the HBM performance gap and captures significant Nvidia HBM3E allocation, or if AI infrastructure capital spending contracts materially (reducing Nvidia GPU volumes and therefore HBM demand).
